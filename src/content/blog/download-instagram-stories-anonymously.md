## Why Instagram makes Stories hard to keep

Stories are designed to be temporary. They disappear from the profile **24 hours** after they are posted, and the app only offers a **Save** option on your own. For anyone else's Story you have three choices: screenshot it, screen record it, or download it by link.

| Method | Quality | Sound | Viewer list | Effort |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Screenshot | Screen size, with interface | None | You appear | One frame at a time |
| Screen recording | Re-compressed, with interface | Yes, if not muted | You appear | Must watch in real time |
| Download by link | Original file | Yes | You do not appear | A few seconds |

Downloading by link is the only way to get the actual video or photo file, without the progress bars and reply box on top.

## How the viewer list works

When you open a Story in the Instagram app, your account is recorded as a viewer, and the owner can see your name for as long as they can see the Story's viewers. That record is made by your account, not by the Story file.

A web downloader like ReelsDl.net never asks for your Instagram login. It requests the public Story on its own, so there is no account of yours involved, and nothing is added to the list. Just remember: if you open the Story in the app first to copy its link, *that* view counts. To stay off the list, copy the link without opening the Story, as described below.

## Download a Story on iPhone or Android

### Copy a link without opening the Story

The cleanest way is to use the account's profile link:

1. In Instagram, go to the profile and tap the **three dots** at the top right.
2. Tap **Copy profile URL**. You get a link like `instagram.com/username`.
3. Change it to `instagram.com/stories/username/` before pasting. That link lists every Story currently on the account.

If you do not mind appearing as a viewer, open the Story, tap the **three dots** at the top right, and choose **Copy link**. That link points at one specific Story.

### Save the file

1. Open [reelsdl.net/stories](/stories) in Safari or Chrome.
2. Paste the link and press **Enter**.
3. Each Story appears with its own button. Tap **Download** to save video Stories as MP4 and photo Stories as JPG.

On **iPhone**, Safari saves files to *Files → Downloads*. Open the file, tap **Share**, and choose **Save Video** or **Save Image** to move it to Photos. On **Android**, files go to the *Download* folder and show up in Gallery or Google Photos.

## Download Instagram Highlights

Highlights are Stories that the owner has pinned to their profile in named circles. They do not expire, so they are often the only place an old Story survives.

1. Tap the Highlight on the profile.
2. Tap the **three dots** and choose **Copy link**. The link looks like `instagram.com/stories/highlights/1789…/`.
3. Paste it into [the Story downloader](/stories).

Every item in the Highlight is listed in order, so you can keep the whole album or just one Story from it.

## What you cannot download

Being clear about the limits saves you time and protects your account:

- **Private accounts.** Their Stories are visible only to approved followers. No legitimate tool can see them without logging in as one.
- **Close Friends Stories.** These carry a green ring and are restricted to a list the owner picked.
- **Expired Stories.** After 24 hours, a Story that is not in a Highlight is gone from Instagram's servers for everyone except the owner, who keeps it in their archive.
- **Stories from blocked or restricted contexts,** such as accounts that have limited who can see them by country or age.

Be wary of any "private Story viewer" that promises otherwise. These sites typically ask you to log in or complete "human verification" surveys, and neither will show you a private Story.

## Saving your own Stories

If the Stories are yours, use Instagram itself:

- **While it is live:** open your Story, tap **More** (three dots), and choose **Save**.
- **After it expires:** go to your profile, open the menu, choose **Archive**, and switch to *Stories archive*. Instagram keeps your Stories there unless you turned archiving off.
- **Everything at once:** *Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information* exports all your Stories, posts, and Reels.

## Be considerate

A public Story is still somebody's work and, often, a glimpse of their personal life. Saving one to remember an event, a recipe, or a sale is normal. Reposting it, especially without credit, or keeping Stories of private individuals to share elsewhere, crosses a line. When in doubt, ask the owner.
