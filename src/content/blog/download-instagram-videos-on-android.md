To download an Instagram video on Android, copy its link in the Instagram app, paste it into the [Instagram video downloader](/) in Chrome, and tap **Download video**. The MP4 is saved to your phone's *Download* folder and appears in Gallery or Google Photos a few seconds later, with its sound and without a watermark. No app is needed.

## What the Instagram app lets you do on Android

Instagram gives you three ways to keep a video, and none of them gives you a clean file:

- **The Download option** in the share menu only appears on public Reels whose creators allow it. The saved video carries the Instagram logo and the creator's username, and some Reels save without their music.
- **Save (the bookmark)** only adds the post to a collection inside Instagram. Nothing is stored on your phone, and the post disappears if the owner deletes it.
- **The screen recorder** in your quick settings records your screen, the app interface, and any notifications that arrive, in real time and at screen resolution.

Downloading by link skips all of that. You get the MP4 Instagram itself streams, in the best quality it offers, in a few seconds.

## Step 1: Copy the video link

1. Open the Reel or video in the Instagram app.
2. Tap the **Share** arrow (paper plane), or the **three dots**.
3. Tap **Copy link**.

The link looks like `instagram.com/reel/…` for a Reel or `instagram.com/p/…` for a video post. Both work, and so do older IGTV links with `/tv/`.

## Samsung Internet, Firefox, and other browsers

Every Android browser saves to the same *Download* folder by default. Only the place you check on progress differs:

| Browser | Where to see the download |
| --- | --- |
| Chrome | Notification bar, or ⋮ menu → **Downloads** |
| Samsung Internet | Notification bar, or ≡ menu → **Downloads** |
| Firefox | Notification bar, or ⋮ menu → **Downloads** |
| Edge, Opera, Brave | Notification bar, or the browser menu → **Downloads** |

If you changed your browser's download location, the file is in that folder instead. Your gallery app may need a moment to scan it.

## Troubleshooting on Android

**The video is not in Gallery.** Open the **Files** app (or *My Files* on Samsung) and go to *Downloads*. If it is there, your gallery app has not scanned the folder yet; restart the app. In Google Photos, look under *Collections → On this device* for the *Download* folder.

**The download fails or never finishes.** Check that you have free storage space and a stable connection, then tap **Download video** again. Long videos take a few seconds longer than short Reels.

**The video plays without sound.** Android has separate ring and media volumes. Press a volume key while the video plays to raise media volume. If it is still silent, Instagram may have muted the music for copyright reasons, which affects every copy of that Reel. More fixes are in [Reels not saving to your camera roll](/blog/instagram-reels-not-saving-to-camera-roll).

**"We could not fetch that link."** The account is private, the post was deleted, or the link was copied incompletely. Open the link in a private Chrome tab: if Instagram asks you to log in to see it, it cannot be downloaded.
