The quickest way to download an Instagram video on an iPhone is to copy its link in Instagram, paste it into the [Instagram video downloader](/) in Safari, and tap **Download**. The video lands in *Files → Downloads*; from there, **Share → Save Video** puts it in your camera roll. No app is needed, the file keeps its sound, and there is no watermark.

## What Instagram lets you do (and what it doesn't)

Instagram has slowly added saving options, but each has a catch:

- **The in-app Download button** only appears on public Reels whose creators allow it, and the saved file carries an Instagram logo and the creator's username. It never appears on ordinary video posts or carousels.
- **The bookmark (Save)** keeps the post in a collection inside Instagram. Nothing is stored on your phone, and the post vanishes if the owner deletes it.
- **Screen recording** captures your screen at screen resolution, includes the interface, and has to run for the full length of the video.

Downloading by link avoids all three problems: you get the actual MP4 Instagram streams, at its best quality, in seconds.

## Step 1: Copy the video link

1. Open the video or Reel in the Instagram app.
2. Tap the **Share** arrow (paper plane) or the **three dots**.
3. Tap **Copy link**.

The link looks like `instagram.com/p/…` for a video post or `instagram.com/reel/…` for a Reel. Both work.

## Step 3: Download and move it to Photos

1. Tap **Download video**. Safari asks whether to download the file; tap **Download**.
2. A blue arrow appears in the address bar. Tap it, then tap the video to open it. Or open the **Files** app and go to **Downloads**.
3. Tap the **Share** icon and choose **Save Video**.

The video is now in the Photos app, in *Recents*, ready to watch offline, trim, or share.

## Using Chrome instead of Safari

The steps are the same, but Chrome on iPhone keeps downloads in its own folder: *Files → On My iPhone → Chrome*. Open the file there and use **Share → Save Video** to move it to Photos. Safari is a little simpler because its downloads button sits in the address bar.

## Troubleshooting on iPhone

**The video does not appear in Photos.** It is still in *Files → Downloads*. Safari never saves straight to Photos; you need the **Save Video** step. More fixes are in [Reels not saving to your camera roll](/blog/instagram-reels-not-saving-to-camera-roll).

**There is no sound.** Check the silent switch (or Action button on newer iPhones) and the volume. If the video still plays silently, Instagram may have muted the music for copyright reasons, which affects every copy.

**"We could not fetch that link."** The account is private, the post was deleted, or the link was copied incompletely. Open the link in a Private tab in Safari: if Instagram asks you to log in to see it, it cannot be downloaded.

**Downloads go to iCloud and take a while.** In *Settings → Apps → Safari → Downloads*, choose **On My iPhone** to save locally instead.
